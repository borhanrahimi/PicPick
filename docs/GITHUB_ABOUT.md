## Short project summary

> PicPick helps users review photos one at a time and organize them into Keep, Trash, and Skip folders. It uses app-private copies to preserve originals and includes undo, zoom, search, settings, and CSV reports. Recruiters can explore the native Android app through Appetize or review the source and screenshots on GitHub.
